⚡ GridVision AI
🤖 Autonomous AI-Powered Power Grid Inspection & Maintenance Intelligence Platform

From aerial inspection to intelligent action. 🚁⚡

GridVision AI is an AI-powered infrastructure monitoring platform designed to help electricity utilities inspect, analyze, prioritize, and manage power-grid assets such as transmission towers and transformers.

Instead of simply detecting infrastructure defects, GridVision AI aims to understand their severity, risk, asset health, and required maintenance action.

🎯 Problem Statement

Power-grid infrastructure requires regular inspection to identify defects before they become serious failures.

Traditional inspection processes can be:

👷 Manual and time-consuming
💰 Expensive to scale
🛰️ Difficult to monitor continuously
📊 Dependent on manual analysis
⚠️ Reactive instead of proactive

There is a need for an intelligent system that can transform inspection data into actionable maintenance decisions.

💡 Our Solution

GridVision AI combines:

🚁 Drone-based Inspection
📸 Computer Vision
🤖 AI-powered Defect Detection
📊 Risk & Severity Analysis

🔧 Maintenance Priority Queue

Instead of treating every detected issue equally, GridVision AI prioritizes maintenance based on risk.

Example:

🔴 P1 → Schedule Maintenance
🟠 P2 → Continue Monitoring
🟡 P3 → Continue Monitoring
🟢 P4 → No Immediate Action

This helps utility teams focus their resources on the infrastructure that requires the most attention.

🧠 AI Decision Intelligence

One of the core concepts of GridVision AI is moving beyond simple defect detection.

Instead of:

❌ "Defect detected."

GridVision AI aims to provide:

✅ "Defect detected → Risk evaluated → Priority assigned → Maintenance action recommended."

This transforms raw inspection data into operational intelligence.
